# Limitations
- Synthetic data. Nothing here is a real company result and no savings are claimed. "Expired" means a synthetic date before the snapshot.
- Heuristic rules; no machine learning, no forecast accuracy claimed. Demand is assumed steady over the last 30 days; seasonality and promotions are ignored.
- Sensitivity: using 90-day demand instead of 30-day moves at-risk value from 26.7% to 26.9%.
- Zero-demand stock is the largest block (INR 8.8M, 11 SKUs) because the generator deliberately includes a few dead SKUs; the size of this block is a designed pattern, not a finding about the real world.
- Transfer flag needs real destination capacity, transport cost and lead time before action.
- Expired or unsafe products must never be sold or used; follow approved disposition procedure.
